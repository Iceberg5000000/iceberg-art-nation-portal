Governance & Legal Compliance

Collector Terms, Intellectual Property & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Studio Entity: Iceberg Art Nation (Founder & Lead Artist: Timothy Stephen Nenji)

1. Intellectual Property & Copyright Retention

All original artworks, digital twins, preparatory sketches, photographs, and brand assets displayed on this portal are protected under the Berne Convention and the Nigerian Copyright Act. Purchasing a physical canvas, graphite drawing, print, or Web3 NFT transfers ownership of the physical object or cryptographic token only.

Timothy Stephen Nenji retains 100% of underlying copyright, moral rights, and commercial reproduction or merchandising rights unless a separate written Commercial Licensing Agreement is executed.

2. Bespoke Commission & Refund Policy

  • Retainer Deposit: A non-refundable 50% studio retainer is required prior to stretching canvas or initiating studio production.
  • Revisions: Up to two (2) structural revisions are included during the preliminary composition/sketch stage. Major changes requested after final painting begins incur additional studio fees.
  • Final Sale: Custom bespoke commissions are created strictly to client specifications and are final sale upon final balance settlement.

3. International Art Freight, Customs & Third-Party Marketplaces

Original works dispatched internationally are packed in museum-grade protective crating or heavy-duty archival tubes and shipped via insured tracked courier (DHL Express / FedEx International). Unless agreed otherwise in writing, the buyer covers insured shipping charges and local customs duties/import VAT.

Purchases completed via Saatchi Art, OpenSea, or Redbubble are governed by those respective marketplaces' buyer protection and fulfillment terms.

4. Collector Privacy & Data Protection

Personal details submitted through commission forms, WhatsApp inquiries, or newsletter signups (name, email, phone number, shipping destination) are collected strictly for order fulfillment, Certificate of Authenticity registration, and collector communications. Iceberg Art Nation never sells or rents personal data to third parties.
